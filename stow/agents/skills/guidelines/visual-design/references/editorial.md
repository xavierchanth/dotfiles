# Editorial

Use the document's established section purposes and content hierarchy as the foundation.
The writing skill owns the argument and wording; visual design makes reading, navigation,
and relationships clear.

Establish a typographic system for sustained reading: body text, heading levels, captions,
notes, quotations, and data. Use spacing and placement to express hierarchy consistently.
Tune line length and leading to the intended viewing size and density.

Shape page rhythm with deliberate transitions between prose, figures, tables, and open
space. Keep headings connected to their content and captions connected to their figures.
Resolve awkward breaks and isolated lines in paginated outputs.

Give complex supporting material enough room to remain useful. Use repeated table
headings and clear label alignment where needed. Read [diagrams](diagrams.md) when
visual relationships support the explanation. Keep figure meanings intact at export size.

Inspect both reading flow and the whole sequence. Verify that navigation, emphasis,
and page composition help readers find and understand material without making every
page visually identical.
