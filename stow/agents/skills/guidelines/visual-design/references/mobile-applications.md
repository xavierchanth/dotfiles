# Mobile Applications

Compose around the current task while keeping supporting capabilities recognizable.
Use platform visual conventions as context for navigation, controls, and state. Express
identity through coherent choices that retain familiar meanings.

Give controls sufficient visual and touch space. Keep labels legible and related
actions clearly grouped. Consider reach, safe areas, text scaling, and the space occupied
by the keyboard when they affect what remains visible and usable.

Use focused regions and progressive detail to handle constrained space. Preserve enough
context for users to understand where they are and what an action affects. Distinguish
persistent navigation from actions local to the current screen.

Establish phone and tablet coverage from the brief. Read
[screen adaptation](screen-adaptation.md) when sizes vary. A wider layout may reveal
related regions together rather than merely enlarge the phone composition. Inspect
representative states, long labels, enlarged text, and keyboard-visible forms.
