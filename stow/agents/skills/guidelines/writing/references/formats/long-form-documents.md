# Long-Form Documents

Establish the argument or explanation and the purpose of each section before drafting.
Support continuity across sections and make depth navigable.

- Thought leadership develops a position and its implications.
- Narrative documents build connected understanding or argument through prose.
- White papers develop substantive explanations with supporting evidence and depth.
- Decision documents connect a decision to relevant alternatives and a recommendation.

Combine these approaches when the reader's task calls for it.
