# Presentations

Establish whether the deck supports a speaker, must stand alone, or needs both forms.
Plan slides around their communicative contribution and the audience's developing
understanding. Distinguish visible copy, speaker notes, supporting detail, and visual
briefs. A diagram brief should explain what the audience must learn from it.

Use investor, sales, or technical guidance according to purpose. Plan transitions
across slides and use appendices for depth that should remain accessible without
interrupting the main sequence. Let visual design determine composition and treatment.
