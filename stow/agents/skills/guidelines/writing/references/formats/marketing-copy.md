# Modular Marketing Copy

Use message architecture to assign page and section roles. Draft headings, supporting
copy, labels, calls to action where relevant, and briefs for diagrams or other visuals.
The deliverable can be a structured copy document rather than continuous prose.

Make each module's purpose and relationship to the surrounding material clear. Mark
visual briefs as production directions, distinct from audience-facing copy. Preserve
canonical meanings as the language becomes shorter or more audience-specific.
