---
name: clarify
description: Use when the user asks for clarity or to reiterate a request to confirm understanding.
---

Reiterate the request in plain language without merely repeating it.

Capture:

- The intended outcome
- Important constraints
- Assumptions or inferred details
- Any ambiguity that could materially change the result

Ask the user to confirm or correct the interpretation. Do not begin the underlying work until they respond.
