Use:

- A tree for hierarchy, nesting, ownership, or structure. Render it as a file-tree-style text block using `├──`, `└──`, and `│`.
- A lo-fi text flowchart for a small, simple flow.
- Mermaid for processes, dependencies, or more complex relationships.

Prefer Mermaid over a lo-fi flowchart when it communicates the relationships more clearly.

```text
root/
├── child-a/
│   └── item
└── child-b/
```
